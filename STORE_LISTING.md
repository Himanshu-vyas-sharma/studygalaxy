## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Forest says "don't touch your phone."
StudyGalaxy says: your friends are studying right now, your Physics planet is dying, and if you quit this session everyone will see the black hole forever.

StudyGalaxy is a focus timer built for students. Every session you complete adds a new star to your personal galaxy. Each subject you study becomes a planet that terraforms over time — from barren rock to rings, moons, atmosphere, life. The longer you focus, the more alive your galaxy becomes.

★ WHY STUDENTS LOVE IT

• LIVE STUDY CIRCLES — Create a circle with your classmates. See who's focusing right now, in real time. When one friend starts studying, the rest feel it. No more lonely study nights.

• PERMANENT PROGRESS — Unlike other focus apps, your galaxy never looks the same. 1 star today, a constellation next week, a terraformed world next month. Progression you can see.

• HONEST FAILURE — Break a focus session and a black hole appears in your galaxy — permanently. Your discipline is visible. So is your growth.

• SUBJECT TRACKING — Tag each session with a subject. See which planets you're neglecting. ("Physics planet is dying — 9 days unseen.")

• SESSION INTENTIONS — Before each session, write one line of what you'll crush. Rate yourself after. Your star's brightness shows how it went.

• STREAKS THAT MATTER — Daily focus streak with a cosmic twist. Miss a day, lose the streak. Compound rewards for consistency.

★ BUILT FOR INDIAN STUDENTS

• Zero data collected beyond what we need — no email, no phone, no address book.
• Works offline. Your galaxy is yours.
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No subscription.
• Low data usage; works on slow connections.
• Zero friction onboarding — pick a cosmic name and start focusing in 10 seconds.

★ HOW IT WORKS

1. Open the app → pick your cosmic name (e.g. NebulaNinja, AstroArjun).
2. Tap the glowing portal → choose 25, 45, or 90 min.
3. Write one line of what you'll study.
4. Focus. If you leave the app for too long, your star collapses.
5. Finish → a new star joins your galaxy forever.
6. Join a friend's circle with a 6-letter code → study together, in real time.

★ WHO IS THIS FOR?

• High school students preparing for board exams.
• College students chasing degrees, grinding GATE / CAT / CS prep.
• Anyone whose phone is their biggest study enemy.
• Study groups that want accountability without a WhatsApp lecture.

★ WHY NOT JUST ANOTHER POMODORO APP?

Because pomodoro timers don't know your friends are studying. StudyGalaxy does. Because they let you quietly fail. StudyGalaxy makes failure visible — and growth visible too. Because they look the same on day 1 and day 100. Your galaxy evolves.

Grow your galaxy. Study with your people. Stop touching your phone.

Download StudyGalaxy and start a 25-minute session right now. Your first star is waiting.

— Built by a student, for students. No corporate parent, no ads, no spying.
```
